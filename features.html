INSIDE MASKSMP

More than a mask.
A whole playstyle.

MaskSMP adds collectible mask equipment, distinct powers and progression to a Minecraft server. Here are the systems included in the project and the milestones that shaped its development.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Built for different
ways to play.

Pick a mask for its identity and powers, then improve it as your player progresses. The visuals are supported by a matching resource pack.

🎭

01 / COLLECTION

16 mask previews

The interactive viewer includes Dragon, Flame, Shadow, Storm, Ocean, Earth, Ice, Void, Nature, Warrior, Speed, Miner, Hunter, Angel, Demon and Galaxy.

Browse the collection →
✦

02 / POWERS

Three powers per mask

Each mask has three abilities, from mobility and utility to attacks and crowd control. Ability keys can work with the compatible Command Keys mod.

Explore mask abilities →
↗

03 / PROGRESSION

Six upgrade tiers

Upgrade masks through six tiers. Costs are controlled by the plugin configuration, so values can depend on the server.

See the upgrade system →
✧

04 / ENCHANTMENTS

Extra customization

Mask enchantments add another layer of customisation, letting players work towards different equipment combinations beyond a mask's base power.

Explore progression →
⚔

05 / SERVER PLAY

SMP and PvP focused

Masks can shape combat choices and player identity in survival, Lifesteal, competitive and creator SMPs. The exact balance depends on the server's rules.

Choose a release →
▧

06 / VISUALS

Custom resource pack

The matching pack supplies mask models and textures, ability GUI icons and custom HUD artwork. Use the matching pack version for the intended in-game design.

Get the resource pack →

PROJECT TIMELINE

From early visuals
to public release.

Milestones from the first idea through the public release and continued development.

7 MAY 2026

The idea begins

I created the MaskSMP Discord server after coming up with the idea, and started researching how to develop the project.

LATE MAY 2026

First steps in plugin development

I began coding MaskSMP and learning how to build a Minecraft plugin for the first time.

JUNE 2026

Code, models and textures

Development continued across the plugin code, mask models and their textures.

EARLY JULY 2026

Halfway through the mask models

By early July, approximately half of the planned mask models had been completed.

24 JULY 2026

15 mask models completed

I completed the original 15 mask models and textures, giving the project its first major visual milestone.

AROUND AUGUST 2026

First public release

I published MaskSMP on Modrinth so other players and server owners could try the plugin.

20 OCTOBER 2026

Development continues

I uploaded another plugin version, continuing development after the initial public release.
