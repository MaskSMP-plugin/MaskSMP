INSIDE MASKSMP

More than a mask.
A whole playstyle.

MaskSMP adds collectible mask equipment, distinct powers and progression to a Minecraft server. Here is what the current project includes, how the systems fit together, and the milestones that shaped its development.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Built for different
ways to play.

Pick a mask for its identity and powers, then improve it as your player progresses. The visuals are supported by a matching resource pack.

🎭

01 / COLLECTION

16 mask previews

The website's interactive mask viewer includes Dragon, Flame, Shadow, Storm, Ocean, Earth, Ice, Void, Nature, Warrior, Speed, Miner, Hunter, Angel, Demon and Galaxy.

Browse the collection →
✦

02 / POWERS

Three powers per mask

Each mask is presented with three abilities and descriptions, from mobility and utility to attacks and crowd control. Ability use can be mapped to keys with the compatible Command Keys mod.

Read mask abilities →
⬆

03 / PROGRESSION

Six upgrade tiers

Players can upgrade masks through a six-tier progression system. Upgrade costs are configured by the plugin; consult the server's plugin configuration for the values used on that server.

Download the plugin →
✨

04 / ENCHANTMENTS

Extra customization

MaskSMP's public project description includes mask enchantments, allowing players to build toward different equipment combinations rather than relying only on a mask's base power.

Get the current release →
🧭

05 / SERVER PLAY

SMP and PvP focused

Masks can shape combat choices and player identity on survival, Lifesteal, competitive and creator SMP servers. Results depend on the server's gameplay rules and configuration.

Compare playstyles →
🎨

06 / VISUALS

Custom resource pack

The matching pack supplies mask models and textures, ability-specific GUI icons, and the custom ability HUD artwork. Use the matching pack version to see the intended design in-game.

PROJECT TIMELINE

From early visuals to public release.

7 MAY 2026

The idea begins

I created the MaskSMP Discord server after coming up with the idea, and started researching how to develop the project.

LATE MAY 2026

First steps in plugin development

I began coding MaskSMP and learning how to build a Minecraft plugin for the first time.

JUNE 2026

Code, models and textures

Development continued across the plugin code, mask models and their textures.

EARLY JULY 2026

Halfway through the mask models

By early July, approximately half of the planned mask models had been completed.

24 JULY 2026

15 mask models completed

I completed the original 15 mask models and textures, giving the project its first major visual milestone.

AROUND AUGUST 2026

First public release

I published MaskSMP on Modrinth so other players and server owners could try the plugin.

20 OCTOBER 2026

Development continues

I uploaded another plugin version, continuing development after the initial public release.
